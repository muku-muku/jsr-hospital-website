What is Palliative Care?

Palliative care is specialized medical support focused on providing relief from pain, stress, and the symptoms of serious illness. Unlike hospice care, palliative care can begin at any stage of an illness and is provided alongside curative treatments.

  • Pain and symptom management
  • Emotional and psychological support
  • Guidance for family members and caregivers
  • Coordination between different medical teams
  • Help with difficult medical decisions

Who Can Benefit from Palliative Care?

Palliative care is appropriate for patients dealing with cancer, heart or lung disease, neurological conditions, kidney or liver disease, and any condition causing chronic pain or distress. It is never too early to ask about palliative care.
