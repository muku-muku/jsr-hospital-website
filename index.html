FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about emergency care, insurance, pharmacy, diagnostics and appointment booking.

Does JSR Hospital have 24x7 emergency services? +
Yes, we provide round-the-clock emergency care with ambulance services available at all times. Call 9870312515 for emergencies.
Which insurance providers are accepted? +
We offer cashless TPA services with 35+ insurance providers including Bajaj Allianz, Aditya Birla Health, IFFCO Tokio, Kotak General, Tata AIG, New India, United India, Oriental, National Insurance, and all leading TPAs.
Is there a pharmacy inside the hospital? +
Yes, we have a 24x7 pharmacy inside the hospital premises for your convenience.
Do you offer home blood sample collection? +
Yes, we offer free blood sample collection from your home. Please call us to schedule a pickup.
How do I book an appointment? +
You can book online using the form above, or call us at 0124-4462995 or 9870312515. Walk-ins are also welcome for OPD.
